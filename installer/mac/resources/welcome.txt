Beatbox Replacer turns beatboxing into MIDI drum notes.

This installs, for all users of this Mac:

  - VST3:        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/BeatboxReplacer.vst3
  - Audio Unit:  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/BeatboxReplacer.component

In Ableton Live, use the VST3 version: Live can't receive MIDI from Audio Unit plug-ins.

If Live is open, quit it before installing.
